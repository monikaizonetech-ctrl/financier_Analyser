## 1. Introduction

The **GST Analyzer** provides a structured and efficient method for uploading, organizing, and reviewing Goods and Services Tax (GST) documents within a unified financial analysis platform.

In modern business environments, managing multiple GST returns, tax invoices, and periodic filing statements across different quarters or entities can create documentation bottlenecks. The GST Analyzer simplifies this workflow by allowing users to upload their GST filing documents and instantly receive a clear, organized summary of key tax parameters, invoice registers, and period-wise trends.

> *The GST Analyzer provides a structured way to upload and review GST-related documents within the same financial analysis platform, enabling teams to organize tax filing information into clear and actionable review summaries.*

---

## 2. Purpose of the Solution

The primary objective of the GST Analyzer is to streamline document handling and make GST filing information easy to navigate, review, and share across teams.

### Core Objectives:
* **Centralized Document Organization:** Keep GST-related documents systematically organized under distinct client or organizational profiles.
* **Simplified Document Review:** Eliminate the need to manually comb through multi-page tax documents to find essential figures such as taxable turnovers or tax liabilities.
* **Structured Information Summaries:** Standardize filing information into clean tables and summary cards that can be understood at a glance.
* **Support for Finance Workflows:** Assist internal finance and bookkeeping teams in collating records, verifying reported numbers, and preparing management reports.

*(Note: The GST Analyzer serves as an organizational and analytical review platform to assist financial teams; it is not a substitute for official government filing portals or licensed statutory tax audits.)*

```

+-------------+-------------+
              |

| 3. Review Uploaded Files  |  Check attached files in the staging view
+-------------+-------------+
              |

+---------------------------+
| 4. Start Analysis         |  Click "Start Analysis" to process and structure the filing data
+-------------+-------------+
              |
              v
+---------------------------+
| 5. View Results           |  Review summary cards, tax breakdowns, and invoice schedules
+-------------+-------------+
              |
              v
+---------------------------+
| 6. Download Reports       |  Export standardized summaries in Excel (.xlsx) and PDF formats
+---------------------------+
```

### Detailed Workflow Description:

1. **Create a GST Analysis Report:**
   The user opens the dashboard, clicks **"New Report"**, enters a descriptive report name (such as the business legal name and filing quarter), and selects **"GST Analyzer"** as the report type.

2. **Upload GST-Related Documents:**
   The user uploads their GST return documents, filing summaries, or tax statement files using the secure upload interface.

3. **Review Uploaded Documents:**
   The interface displays the uploaded file list, allowing the user to review file names, confirm document completeness, or remove files before proceeding.

4. **Start the Analysis:**
   The user clicks **"Start Analysis"**. The system processes the document information and structures the records into a comprehensive analysis dataset.

5. **View the Results:**
   The system displays the processed results, including business identification details, filing status, aggregate taxable values, tax liability distributions, and monthly trends.

6. **Download Review Reports:**
   The user can download the finalized analysis in professional **Excel** or **PDF** formats for documentation and distribution.

## 5. Result and Document Review Experience

The platform provides a clean and structured layout for reviewing GST information:

* **File-Level Summary Cards:** Immediate display of detected entity identification (GSTIN, Legal Name) and return filing period.
* **Tax Component Overview:** A clear side-by-side comparison of IGST (inter-state), CGST (central), and SGST (state) amounts, enabling quick verification of tax splits.
* **Invoice Schedules:** Organized tabular views detailing individual invoices, categorizing them by business-to-business (B2B), business-to-consumer (B2C), and adjustment entries (Credit Notes).
* **Trend Analysis:** Month-by-month financial summary highlighting peaks and troughs in taxable turnover and tax obligations.
